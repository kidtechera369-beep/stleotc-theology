Glossary of Key Terms

Theological terms used across our lessons — from Ge'ez words of our Tewahedo heritage to the language of the Councils. Each entry links to the lesson where it is taught.

A

Abiy Tsom
The Great Lent — the longest and most solemn fast, preparing for the Feast of the Resurrection. — from The Purpose and Practice of Fasting
Absolution
The loosing of sins through the priest's prayer, by the authority Christ gave (Matt 16:19; John 20:23). — from The Sacrament of Penance
Apostolic See
A city-church founded by an Apostle or his direct disciple, e.g., Alexandria, Antioch, Rome. — from The Ancient Faith: Early Church Fathers and the Path of the Tewahedo Church
Asceticism
Spiritual training through self-denial — for love of God, never for pride. — from The Purpose and Practice of Fasting

B

Begotten
The Son's eternal relationship to the Father — coming forth as a word from the mind, outside of time, never created. — from Lesson on the Holy Trinity
Body of Christ
The Church as a living organism whose life flows from Christ the Head. — from The Book of Ephesians

C

Chalcedon
The 451 AD council whose 'in two natures' formula the Oriental Orthodox churches rejected. — from The Ancient Faith: Early Church Fathers and the Path of the Tewahedo Church
Compassion
Stepping into another's shoes; the love that kills judgment. — from Iron Sharpens Iron
Confession
Naming our sins sincerely before God in the presence of His priest. — from The Sacrament of Penance
Contentment
Learned steadiness in every season — abundance or need — through Christ who strengthens (Phil 4:11–13). — from The Book of Philippians
Crowning
The visible rite of Orthodox marriage, crowning the couple into their new life in Christ. — from The Sacrament of Holy Matrimony

D

Deacon (Diakon)
The first rank of the ordained ministry, serving at the altar and in teaching — as Dn Yonnas serves this class.
Debre Libanos
The great monastery founded by St. Tekle Haimanot in Shewa, c. 1284. — from The Life of Saint Tekle Haimanot

E

Encyclical
A letter intended to circulate among many churches, not just one congregation. — from The Book of Ephesians
Epitimia
A spiritual discipline given by the confessor — medicine for healing, not punishment. — from The Sacrament of Penance
Essence (Nature)
The shared divine substance of God — what makes God, God. There is only one, indivisible. — from Lesson on the Holy Trinity
Eucharist
The sacrament of the true Body and Blood of the one Christ, received in the Kidassie.
Evangelist
A writer of one of the four Holy Gospels. — from The Life and Legacy of St. Luke the Apostle

F

Fasika
The Feast of the Resurrection (Easter) — the victory of Christ over death. — from Great Lent and the Victory of the Resurrection
Father Confessor (Yenefs Abat)
The priest who hears confession and shepherds the soul's healing. — from The Sacrament of Penance
Fellowship
The shared life of believers who sharpen one another in love. — from Iron Sharpens Iron
Filseta
The Fast of the Assumption of St. Mary, beloved especially in the Ethiopian Church. — from The Purpose and Practice of Fasting

G

Gates of the soul
The senses — what we watch, hear, and touch enters and shapes the heart. — from Guarding Our Time and Senses: Using Media Wisely
Ge'ez
The ancient liturgical language of the Ethiopian Church; 'Tewahedo' is a Ge'ez word meaning 'made one.' — from The Ancient Faith: Early Church Fathers and the Path of the Tewahedo Church
Golgotha
The place of the Crucifixion — where the Devil was defeated. — from The Holy Cross: History, Significance, and Doctrine
Grace
God's own life and power given to heal and transform — creating obedience, not canceling it. — from The Book of Ephesians
Gädl
The written 'spiritual combat' or life-story of a saint, e.g., the Gädlä Täklä Haymanot. — from The Life of Saint Tekle Haimanot

H

Hermit
A monk who withdraws into solitude for unceasing prayer. — from The Life of Saint Tekle Haimanot
Holy Tradition
The living faith handed down in the Church — distinct from human customs or outside philosophies. — from The Book of Colossians
Hosanna
Palm Sunday — the Lord's entry into Jerusalem, opening Holy Week. — from Great Lent and the Victory of the Resurrection
Hypostasis
A Greek theological term for a real personal distinction within the one God — a distinct personal reality, not a separate god. — from Trinitarianism and Monotheism: One God in Three Hypostases

I

Iconographer
A painter of holy icons; tradition remembers St. Luke as the first, painting the Theotokos. — from The Life and Legacy of St. Luke the Apostle
Intercession
Prayer offered on behalf of another — commanded and honored by God throughout Scripture. — from Intercession of the Saints
Invisible Grace
The real divine gift granted through the sacrament, beyond what eyes can see. — from The Sacrament of Holy Matrimony

J

Justification
In the Orthodox reading: not only a legal status but the healing and restoration of our broken nature in Christ. — from The Book of Romans

K

Kenosis
Christ's self-emptying (Phil 2:7): taking the form of a servant, obedient even to death on the cross. — from The Book of Philippians
Kidassie
The Divine Liturgy of the Ethiopian Orthodox Tewahedo Church, in which the faith of the one united Christ is confessed and the Holy Eucharist is celebrated.

L

Long-suffering
The patient endurance that Scripture counts among the fruit of the Spirit (Gal 5:22). — from Patience

M

Martyr
A witness unto death; St. Stephen (Acts 6–7) is the first martyr of the Church. — from The Book of Acts
Medhanit
Savior / Healer — Christ as the Great Physician who restores human nature. — from The Book of Romans
Mediator
In the unique propitiatory sense, Christ alone; in the sense of praying for others, all the righteous. — from Intercession of the Saints
Meskel
The Ethiopian feast of the Finding of the True Cross (Meskerem 17), celebrated with the Demera bonfire. — from The Holy Cross: History, Significance, and Doctrine
Mia Physis
St. Cyril's formula: 'one incarnate nature of God the Word' — unity without confusion or division. — from St. Cyril of Alexandria: The Seal of the Fathers
Miaphysitism
The Oriental Orthodox confession of one united incarnate nature of God the Word — not 'monophysitism.' — from The Ancient Faith: Early Church Fathers and the Path of the Tewahedo Church
Monasticism
The consecrated life of prayer, fasting, and renunciation for the Kingdom of God. — from The Life of Saint Tekle Haimanot
Monotheism
Belief in one God. Orthodox Trinitarian faith is fully monotheistic. — from Trinitarianism and Monotheism: One God in Three Hypostases

N

Nestorianism
The condemned teaching that Christ is two separate persons, one divine and one human. — from St. Cyril of Alexandria: The Seal of the Fathers

O

Oriental Orthodox
The family of churches — Ethiopian, Eritrean, Coptic, Syriac, Armenian, and Indian — holding the first three Ecumenical Councils and the Miaphysite confession.
Outward Sign
What we can see and hear in a sacrament — words, actions, and elements. — from The Sacrament of Holy Matrimony

P

Pentecost
The descent of the Holy Spirit upon the Apostles (Acts 2), fifty days after the Resurrection — the birth of the Church's public witness. — from The Book of Acts
Proceeding
The Holy Spirit's eternal relationship to the Father — not created, not begotten, eternally proceeding. — from Lesson on the Holy Trinity
Propitiation
The payment for sin. Christ alone is the propitiation for our sins (1 John 2:1–2). — from Intercession of the Saints
Providence
God's wise care ordering all things — including the timing we do not choose. — from Patience

R

Redeeming the time
Using the unrepeatable gift of time purposefully for God (Eph 5:16). — from Guarding Our Time and Senses: Using Media Wisely

S

Sacrament (Mystery)
A holy act through which invisible divine grace is given by means of a visible sign. — from The Sacrament of Holy Matrimony
Sanctification
The lifelong recovery and growth in holiness that follows baptism, led by the Holy Spirit. — from The Book of Romans
Sign of the Cross
The believer's act of blessing and protection, confessing the Crucified in body and soul. — from The Holy Cross: History, Significance, and Doctrine
Supremacy of Christ
Christ as Creator, Sustainer, Head of the Church, and fullness of God (Col 1:15–18; 2:9). — from The Book of Colossians
Synaxarium
The Church's book of the saints, read according to the day of the year; the Ethiopic Synaxarium was translated by E. A. Wallis Budge as The Book of the Saints of the Ethiopian Church.
Synergy
Cooperation between God's grace and our free response — faith working through love. — from The Book of Romans
Synod (Council)
The gathered Church deciding matters of faith together, as in Acts 15 — the opposite of private interpretation. — from The Book of Acts

T

Tewahedo
Ge'ez for 'made one' — the perfect union of divinity and humanity in the one Christ. — from St. Cyril of Alexandria: The Seal of the Fathers
The 5,500-year promise
The Church's tradition of God's covenant with Adam: that after 5,500 years the Savior would come and restore him. — from Love and Forgiveness: The Transforming Cycle of Grace
The Nine Saints
Monastic missionaries who came to Ethiopia after Chalcedon and founded her great monasteries. — from The Ancient Faith: Early Church Fathers and the Path of the Tewahedo Church
The Seventy-Two
The wider circle of disciples sent by the Lord (Luke 10); tradition counts St. Luke among them. — from The Life and Legacy of St. Luke the Apostle
Theosis
Becoming by grace what God is by nature — the goal of salvation, in which the saints already shine. — from Intercession of the Saints
Theotokos
Greek for 'God-bearer' / Mother of God — the title of St. Mary defended at Ephesus. — from St. Cyril of Alexandria: The Seal of the Fathers
Trinity
The one God eternally existing as Father, Son, and Holy Spirit — one in essence, three in Hypostases. — from Trinitarianism and Monotheism: One God in Three Hypostases
True Cross
The actual cross of the Crucifixion, uncovered by Queen Helena in Jerusalem. — from The Holy Cross: History, Significance, and Doctrine
Tsega
Grace — in Oriental Orthodox understanding, the divine energy and life-giving power of God, not a mere concept. — from The Book of Acts
Tsom
Fast / fasting — the Church's school of self-denial, prayer, and return to God. — from The Purpose and Practice of Fasting

U

Unbegotten
The Father's personal property: He is the Source (Arche), proceeding from no one. — from Lesson on the Holy Trinity

V

Vain philosophy
Teaching that sounds wise and spiritual but pulls believers away from Christ's sufficiency (Col 2:8). — from The Book of Colossians

W

Watchfulness (Nipsis)
The sober guarding of heart and senses taught by the Fathers — spiritual alertness. — from Guarding Our Time and Senses: Using Media Wisely
