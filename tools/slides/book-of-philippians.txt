Philippians

--- Slide 2 ---
Context and Background
Author:
St. Paul the Apostle (with Timothy in the greeting) —
Phil 1:1
When:
During Paul’s imprisonment (commonly placed around
A.D. 60–62
)
Where:
Traditionally associated with
Rome
(though the key point is:
written in chains
) —
Phil 1:7; 1:12–14
Audience:
The Church in Philippi, faithful partners in the Gospel —
Phil 4:15–16
Tone:
joy and encouragement under pressure —
Phil 1:3–5; 4:4
Paul is suffering, yet this is one of the most joyful letters in the Bible.
Scripture :
Philippians 1:7 (NKJV)
— “…inasmuch as both in my chains and in the defense and confirmation of the gospel, you all are partakers with me of grace.”
--- Slide 3 ---
The Big Message of
Philippians
Joy is not the absence of pain
Joy is the
presence
of Christ
Philippians teaches how to live with:
pressure
conflict
anxiety
spiritual struggle
Philippians
is about a stable heart in an unstable world.
Scripture:
Philippians 4:7 (NKJV)
— “And the peace of God, which surpasses all understanding, will guard your hearts and minds through Christ Jesus.”
--- Slide 4 ---
Chapter 1: Joy in Chains
“What happened to Paul actually helped the Gospel”
Paul’s imprisonment looked like a setback
God used it to spread the Gospel
Even suffering became a witness
What feels like a prison in your life can become a platform for God.
Scripture:
Philippians 1:12 (NKJV)
— “But I want you to know, brethren, that the things which happened to me have actually turned out for the furtherance of the gospel.”
--- Slide 5 ---
Chapter 1: “To Live In Christ”
Paul’s life is centered on Christ, not self
Death is not ultimate loss for the believer
This gives freedom from fear
If Christ is your life, fear loses power over you.
Scripture:
Philippians 1:21 (NKJV)
— “For to me, to live is Christ, and to die is gain.”
--- Slide 6 ---
Chapter 1: Stand Together
Paul calls the church to one spirit and one purpose
Unity is part of Christian witness
A divided church weakens its testimony
Joy and unity belong together.
Scripture:
Philippians 1:27 (NKJV)
— “Only let your conduct be worthy of the gospel of Christ… standing fast in one spirit, with one mind striving together for the faith of the gospel.”
--- Slide 7 ---
Chapter 2: The Mind of Christ
The center of the whole lesson
“Let this mind be in you…”
Christianity is not only belief; it is formation
We are called to think, love, and live like Christ
The Christian life is not just “what you believe about Jesus,” but “what kind of person you are becoming.”
Scripture:
Philippians 2:5 (NKJV)
— “Let this mind be in you which was also in Christ Jesus.”
--- Slide 8 ---
Chapter 2: Humility vs Pride
Do nothing through selfish ambition
Count others as more important than yourself
Pride destroys peace faster than persecution
The greatest enemy of unity is often not pressure outside the Church, but pride inside the heart.
Scripture:
Philippians 2:3 (NKJV)
— “Let nothing be done through selfish ambition or conceit, but in lowliness of mind let each esteem others better than himself.”
--- Slide 9 ---
Chapter 2: Christ’s Humility and Glory
Christ emptied Himself
Took the form of a servant
Became obedient to death, even the cross
Then God highly exalted Him
In the Kingdom of God, humility is not weakness — it is the road to glory.
Scripture:
Philippians 2:8–9 (NKJV)
— “And being found in appearance as a man, He humbled Himself and became obedient to the point of death, even the death of the cross. Therefore God also has highly exalted Him and given Him the name which is above every name.”
--- Slide 10 ---
Chapter 2: Work Out Your Salvation
Paul says: “work out your salvation”
This does not mean “save yourself”
It means: cooperate with the grace God is already working in you
Orthodox lens:
God works in us; we respond. This is spiritual cooperation (synergy).
Scripture:
Philippians 2:12–13 (NKJV)
— “…work out your own salvation with fear and trembling; for it is God who works in you both to will and to do for His good pleasure.”
--- Slide 11 ---
Chapter 2: Shine Without Complaining
Complaining and arguing poison community life
Paul calls believers to shine as lights
Holiness shows up in speech, attitude, and daily behavior
A holy life is not only big moments. It is also how you speak when you’re frustrated.
Scripture:
Philippians 2:14–15 (NKJV)
— “Do all things without complaining and disputing, that you may become blameless and harmless, children of God without fault… among whom you shine as lights in the world.”
--- Slide 12 ---
Chapter 2: Guard Against False Confidence
Paul warns against confidence in the flesh
Religious status, background, and achievements cannot replace Christ
Pride can wear a religious costume
You can be “religious” and still far from Christ if your trust is in yourself.
Scripture:
Philippians 3:3 (NKJV)
— “For we are the circumcision, who worship God in the Spirit, rejoice in Christ Jesus, and have no confidence in the flesh.”
--- Slide 13 ---
Chapter 2: Loss and Gain
Paul counts his old status as loss
Knowing Christ is worth more than everything else
Christ is the treasure, not just the helper
The Christian life matures when Christ stops being an “add-on” and becomes everything.
Scripture:
Philippians 3:8 (NKJV)
— “Yet indeed I also count all things loss for the excellence of the knowledge of Christ Jesus my Lord…”
--- Slide 14 ---
Chapter 3: Press Toward the Prize
Paul says he has not already arrived
He forgets what is behind and presses forward
Spiritual life is a race, not a one-time moment
A real disciple is not perfect — a real disciple keeps moving toward Christ.
Scripture:
Philippians 3:13–14 (NKJV)
— “Brethren, I do not count myself to have apprehended; but one thing I do, forgetting those things which are behind and reaching forward… I press toward the goal for the prize of the upward call of God in Christ Jesus.”
--- Slide 15 ---
Chapter 3:
Citizenship
in Heaven
Christians live on earth but belong to heaven
Our identity shapes our choices
Hope in Christ gives endurance
If heaven is your homeland, this world will not define your values.
Scripture:
Philippians 3:20 (NKJV)
— “For our citizenship is in heaven, from which we also eagerly wait for the Savior, the Lord Jesus Christ.”
--- Slide 16 ---
Chapter 4: Stand Firm and Heal Conflict
Paul calls the church to stand firm
He addresses conflict directly (Euodia and Syntyche)
Spiritual maturity includes reconciliation
Peace in the Church must be protected on purpose.
Scripture:
Philippians 4:2–3 (NKJV)
— “I implore Euodia and I implore Syntyche to be of the same mind in the Lord… help these women who labored with me in the gospel…”
--- Slide 17 ---
Chapter 4: Rejoice, Pray, and Guard the Mind
Rejoice always
Bring everything to God in prayer
Thanksgiving protects the heart
God’s peace guards mind and heart
Prayer is not the last option. It is the first defense against anxiety.
Scripture:
Philippians 4:6–7 (NKJV)
— “Be anxious for nothing, but in everything by prayer and supplication, with thanksgiving, let your requests be made known to God; and the peace of God… will guard your hearts and minds through Christ Jesus.”
--- Slide 18 ---
Chapter 4: Think on What is Holy
Paul gives a filter for the mind:
true
honorable
just
pure
lovely
Thought life shapes spiritual life
What you repeatedly think about, you slowly become.
Scripture:
Philippians 4:8 (NKJV)
— “Finally, brethren, whatever things are true… noble… just… pure… lovely… meditate on these things.”
--- Slide 19 ---
Chapter 4: Contentment In Every Season
Paul learned contentment in lack and abundance
“I can do all things” means endurance through every condition
Strength in Christ is for faithfulness, not ego
Contentment is learned. It does not come naturally.
Scripture:
Philippians 4:11–13 (NKJV)
— “…for I have learned in whatever state I am, to be content… I can do all things through Christ who strengthens me.”
--- Slide 20 ---
Final Summary and Challenge
Philippians in one sentence:
Joy in suffering, humility in Christ, pursuit of holiness, and peace through prayer.
Final challenge
What is stealing your joy right now?
Where is pride blocking peace?
What do you need to bring to God in prayer this week?
Philippians teaches us that joy is not the absence of struggle — it is Christ in the middle of it.
Scripture:
Philippians 4:13 (NKJV)
— “I can do all things through Christ who strengthens me.”
