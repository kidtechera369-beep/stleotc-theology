--- Slide 2 ---
Who Was St. Cyril (Abba Kirillos)?
The Pillar of Faith
• 24th Pope and Patriarch of Alexandria (412–444 AD)
• Nephew of Theophilus of Alexandria, a powerful defender of the Orthodox faith
• Raised and trained within Church leadership
The Connection:
• Alexandria is the Mother Church of Ethiopia
• Our faith flows through his teachings
Legacy:
• Called “The Seal of the Fathers”
• He preserved and completed the teachings before him
--- Slide 3 ---
The Foundation Of Our Salvation
The Question:
• Is Jesus Christ one person or two?
• Is He God, man, or both?
The Tewahedo Answer:
• He is one united nature (Tewahedo) and one person — the incarnate Word of God. Fully Go
d and fully Man.
Why it matters:
• If Christ is divided, His suffering cannot save us
• To know Christ correctly is to know God correctly

• Is He divided?
• Is He one?

This question led to a major theological conflict
The Nestorian Controversy
The Christological Controversy of the 5th Century
The Debate over the Nature of Christ
--- Slide 5 ---
Who Was Nestorius?
The Bishop of Constantinople
• Patriarch of Constantinople (one of the highest Church positions)
• Influential preacher and teacher
• His teachings spread quickly across the empire
His Approach:
• Relied heavily on human reasoning
• Struggled to accept the mystery of God becoming man
The 5 Ancient Church Sees (The Pentarchy)
Rome
Constantinople
Alexandria
Antioch
Jerusalem
--- Slide 6 ---
Nestorius’ False Argument
The “Two-Person” Error
• Christ is two separate persons:
• One human (Jesus)
• One divine (Son of God)
• God only “dwelt in” the man Jesus
Like a person living in a house
Key Problem:
This creates two Sons instead of one Christ
--- Slide 7 ---
The Rejection of Theotokos
Theotokos
(Mother of God)
• Nestorius rejected the title Theotokos
His claim:
• Mary gave birth only to the human Jesus
The danger:
If Mary is not the Mother of God, then the one born is not truly God
This divides Christ
--- Slide 8 ---
The Problem with a Divided Christ
Why Nestorius Was Condemned
• A “mere man” cannot save the world
• A “mere man” cannot forgive sins
If God did not truly suffer for us, we are not truly saved
Tewahedo Truth:
• God the Word suffered in the flesh for us
--- Slide 9 ---
St. Cyril’s Argument
The Miracle of the Union (Tewahedo)
Christ is ONE — not two
• Fully God
• Fully man
The Incarnation:
• The Word did not visit a man
• The Word became man
One unified Christ
--- Slide 10 ---
The Analogy of Iron and Fire
Understanding Tewahedo
• Iron placed in fire becomes red-hot
Unity:
• The iron and fire are united and act as one
No confusion:
• Fire does not become iron
• Iron does not become fire
No separation:
• You cannot strike the iron without touching the heat
This is Christ:
One united reality out of two

Mary is the Mother of God (Theotokos)
Key Truth:
Honoring Mary protects the truth about Christ
