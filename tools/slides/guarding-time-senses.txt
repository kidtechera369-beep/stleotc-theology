--- Slide 2 ---
Today’s Class: What We Will Learn
• Media has power: it shapes our thoughts, habits, desires, and behavior.
• Our senses are spiritual gates: what we watch, hear, and scroll can affect the heart.
• Time is a gift from God: once it passes, it cannot be recovered.
• Not all content is beneficial: Christians must be selective and spiritually awake.
• God must receive our best time: not only what is left over.
• We are called to holiness: even in how we use technology and entertainment.

Opening Discussion
Let’s be honest

• Do you control your screen time, or does your screen time control you?

The Danger of Wasted Time
Wasted time becomes dangerous when it replaces prayer, Scripture, service, family, study, and spiritual growth.
• Endless scrolling
• Binge-watching
• Gaming without limits
• Watching random videos for hours
• Following celebrity drama
• Talking online with no purpose
• Staying up late consuming useless content

God Deserves Time Daily
• Many people say, “I do not have time to pray.”
• But often we have time for social media, movies, shows, music, games, group chats, and random scrolling.
• The question is not only: “Do I have time?”
• The real question is: “What am I choosing to give my time to?”

Giving God the First and Best
• God should not receive only our leftover time.
• A spiritual person gives God priority.
• Set time for prayer.
• Set time for Scripture and spiritual reading.
• Set time for Church, confession, repentance, service, and silence with God.
• If God is first in our life, He must also be first in our schedule.

Prioritize God Before Distractions
• Many people finish everything else and then give God whatever energy remains.
• By the end of the day, they are too tired to pray or read Scripture.
• Do not give God the weakest part of your day.
• Give Him time when your mind is awake, your heart is attentive, and your spirit is ready.

• How much time did I spend with God?
• How much time did I spend on entertainment?
• What content affected my thoughts the most?

--- Slide 21 ---
How to Avoid the Destructive Impact of Media
• Fill your free time with God and His Word.
• Set limits on entertainment and scrolling.
• Be selective about what you watch and hear.
• Avoid content that weakens purity.
• Replace harmful content with spiritual content.
• Do not browse without purpose.
• Protect your heart before small sins become strong habits.

--- Slide 22 ---
A Christian Media Filter
If it weakens your soul, it is not worth your time.
• Is it pure?
• Is it useful?
• Is it controlling me?
• Is it wasting time?
• Is it leading me toward sin?
• Is it helping me grow?
• Would I be comfortable bringing this before God?
Week 32 & 34 lesson sources | Orthodox Study Bible | Fr. Tadros Y. Malaty
--- Slide 23 ---
The Orthodox Way of Using Technology
• Technology should be a servant, not a master.
• Use media for learning, communication, ministry, encouragement, spiritual growth, evangelism, creativity, and work.
• Never allow technology to rule your thoughts.
• Never allow technology to steal your peace.
• Never allow technology to pollute your senses.
• Never allow technology to replace your relationship with God.
Week 32 & 34 lesson sources | Orthodox Study Bible | Fr. Tadros Y. Malaty
--- Slide 24 ---
Practical Application: This Week’s Challenge
The 7-Day Guard Your Time Challenge: reduce bad habits and replace them with holy habits.
• Limit unnecessary screen time.
• Avoid one type of content that weakens your purity.
• Spend at least 10 minutes daily in prayer.
• Read one passage of Scripture each day.
• Replace one entertainment habit with something spiritually useful.
• Track how much time you spend with God compared to media.

Conclusion
• TV, the internet, and media have a powerful impact on our lives, thoughts, behavior, and spiritual condition.
• As Orthodox Christians, we are called to be awake, selective, pure, and wise.
• We must guard our senses, protect our hearts, and redeem the time God has given us.
Use media without being ruled by it.
Use time without wasting it.
Live holy, because God is holy.
