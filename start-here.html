Start Here: A Path Through the Lessons

New to the class, or want to study in order? Follow this path. It moves from the foundations of the faith, through the story and life of the Church, into the Scriptures, the saints, and daily Christian living. Take your time — these lessons are meant to be prayed as much as studied.

  1. The Faith of the Church

    Begin with who God is — one God in three Persons — and who Christ is.

    Lesson on the Holy Trinity · Trinitarianism and Monotheism: One God in Three Hypostases · St. Cyril of Alexandria: The Seal of the Fathers

  2. The Story of the Church

    How the faith came down to us: the Fathers, the councils, and the Cross.

    The Ancient Faith: Early Church Fathers and the Path of the Tewahedo Church · The Holy Cross: History, Significance, and Doctrine

  3. The Life of the Church

    The holy mysteries and disciplines that heal and feed the believer.

    The Sacrament of Penance · The Sacrament of Holy Matrimony · The Purpose and Practice of Fasting

  4. The Scriptures

    Walk through the New Testament with the mind of the Church.

    The Book of Acts · The Book of Romans · The Book of Ephesians · The Book of Philippians · The Book of Colossians

  5. The Saints and Their Prayers

    The cloud of witnesses who intercede for us and show us the way.

    The Life and Legacy of St. Luke the Apostle · The Life of Saint Tekle Haimanot · Intercession of the Saints

  6. Living the Faith Daily

    Practical lessons for the spiritual life of a young Orthodox Christian.

    Guarding Our Time and Senses: Using Media Wisely · Iron Sharpens Iron · Patience

About These Lessons

Lessons marked with a week number follow the Sunday School Curriculum in English, Level V (High School and College), compiled by Kesis Solomon Mulugeta Zewde (PhD) for Debre Nazareth St. Mary & St. Gabriel EOTC. Saints' lives draw on the Ethiopic Synaxarium (The Book of the Saints of the Ethiopian Church). Full citations appear at the bottom of every lesson page.
