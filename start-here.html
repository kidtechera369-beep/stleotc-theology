Start Here

Welcome — this is a place of knowledge in the Ethiopian Orthodox Tewahedo faith: in-depth lessons for young adults and the full Sunday School curriculum for every age. Here is how to make the most of it, whoever you are.

  1. Browse the Lesson Library

    Find lessons by topic. Each one has a summary, Scripture references, key terms, and a full reading.

  2. Read & reflect

    Read the lesson slowly. Look up the Scriptures and study the key terms within the lesson.

  3. Test yourself

    Take the short self-test at the end of each lesson to check your understanding.

  4. Download & review

    Grab the slides and reading materials from the Download Center to study or share.

  5. Ask your priest

    For any doctrinal question, always consult your parish priest.
